# Higher-Order Homogeneous Linear Differential Equations with Constant Coefficients

## 1. Introduction and Theoretical Foundations
Formal definition of higher-order homogeneous linear differential equations with constant coefficients. It covers the historical context, mathematical evolution, and their fundamental importance in fields such as mechanics, physics, and biology.

## 2. Auxiliary Equation Method and Root Analysis
Mathematical resolution by transforming calculus into algebra using the assumption of an exponential solution ($y = e^{rx}$). This section includes the analysis and graphical representation of the three possible cases: 
* Distinct real roots
* Repeated real roots
* Complex roots

## 3. Physical Simulations and Virtual Laboratory
Application of mathematical theory to real physical systems in engineering. It explores the ideal mass-spring system, the behavior of the damped oscillator (with varying friction), and energy analysis through the phase space graph.

## 4. Practical Methodology and Assessment
Synthesis of the resolution process through a structured four-step guide and a summary cheat sheet. It concludes with the application of the acquired knowledge through interactive problem-solving dynamics and practical challenges.
